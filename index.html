About

Welcome to my page! I am an élève normalien at the École Normale Supérieure de Rennes, in the Department of Mathematics. I am also a machine learning researcher at Centre Borelli, ENS Paris-Saclay.

My research focuses on probabilistic and geometric methods for machine learning, high-dimensional statistics, density analysis, optimal transport, graph-based learning, and statistical learning theory.
