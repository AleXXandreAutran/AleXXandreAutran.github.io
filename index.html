I am a student in the Mathematics Department of the École normale supérieure de Rennes. Since September 2026, I have been a research intern in machine learning at the Centre Borelli of the ENS Paris-Saclay.

I am interested in stochastic processes, statistics, optimization, and their applications to machine learning (such as clustering and representation learning). I enjoy understanding the theoretical foundations of these fields while also exploring their numerical and computational aspects.

Outside the lab and studies, I enjoy doing sports, especially running, cycling, and strength training.
